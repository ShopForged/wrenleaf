Privacy Policy

Wrenleaf ShopForge · Effective date: September 25, 2026

Wrenleaf ShopForge ("the app") is a private tool used by one person — the owner of the Wrenleaf Studio shop on Etsy — to manage that shop's own listings and to publish that shop's own product images to its own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What the app accesses

  • Etsy (owner's shop only): the shop's listings, listing images and files, listing statistics, and the shop's own order receipts, used to publish products and count sales. Buyer names and addresses in receipts are not copied into the app's records beyond an order ID, date, item and amount.
  • Pinterest (owner's account only): the owner's boards, Pins, basic account info (username) and Pin analytics, used to publish the shop's product Pins and see which ones perform.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access any other Etsy seller's or Pinterest user's private data.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use cookies, trackers or analytics on visitors, and it has no public website that collects data.
  • It does not use data from Etsy or Pinterest to train AI models.

Where data is stored

All data, including OAuth access and refresh tokens, is stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to third-party servers except the API calls made directly to Etsy and Pinterest to perform the actions above.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active and are deleted when the owner disconnects the account in the app. Access can also be revoked at any time from Etsy (Account Settings → Apps) or Pinterest (Settings → Security / Apps). Sales statistics are kept for the owner's own bookkeeping and deleted on request or when the tool is retired.

Buyers and visitors

If you buy from Wrenleaf Studio, your purchase is handled by Etsy under Etsy's Privacy Policy. If you view our Pins, Pinterest's Privacy Policy applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
